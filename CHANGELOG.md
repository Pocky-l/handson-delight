## [0.1.0-beta.1+1.20.1]

First beta. Only the cooking pot is reworked so far.

- The cooking pot opens right in the world. Put food into the water, stir it with the spoon and take the soup out with a
  bowl.
- Nothing cooks without stirring. Hoppers can still fill the pot.
- A recipe book hangs next to the pot and puts what you can cook now first.
- Players nearby see the soup and the spoon.
